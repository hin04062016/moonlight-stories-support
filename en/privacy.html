Privacy Policy

Updated October 23, 2026 · Applies to the ad-free version 1.4.3

小小故事屋 provides story time without ads, tracking, or third-party analytics in this version. No reader account is required.

Operator and contact

小小故事屋 is operated by Yu Hin Lai. Parents or guardians can contact hinsstudio2023@gmail.com for support, privacy questions, or deletion requests. Please do not include a child's name, photos, address, or other unnecessary personal information.

Reading data on your device

Favorites, reading progress, language, appearance, narrator, and volume preferences are stored on your current device. The app does not send this information to us. We do not operate reader accounts, cloud synchronization, or reading analytics. Your device's system backups may include app data, subject to your Apple settings and the relevant service policies.

Prepared stories and narration

Stories, illustrations, narration, music, and sound effects are included in the app and work offline. The developer uses Google Cloud speech services to prepare narration before distribution. Readers do not send reading content, voice recordings, or personal information to that service through the app. The app does not require microphone, camera, contacts, or location permissions.

No advertising or tracking

This version displays no ads and includes no Google AdMob or other advertising SDK. It does not access advertising identifiers or include third-party behavioral analytics or tracking services. We do not sell reading data or share it with advertisers.

Optional support from a parent

The contact action is behind a parental gate. This gate restricts an external contact action; it is not identity verification or a legal parental-consent process. The app does not automatically send email, attach reading history, or upload diagnostics. If a parent chooses to email us through their email service, we receive the sender's address and the content they provide to respond to that request. Email providers also process messages under their own policies. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the issue or as required by applicable law. You may request access, correction, or deletion, except where retention is legally required.

Deletion and children's privacy

Children are not asked to register or provide personal information. Deleting the app removes its local data from the current device; offloading it may retain data. System backups are managed through Apple's backup settings. We cannot remotely access or delete data on your device. If a parent accidentally includes a child's information in a support message, please contact us to request its deletion.

Policy changes

Features that change data processing will be accompanied by an updated policy and any required choices or consent in the applicable version. This policy describes the current ad-free version and does not provide consent for advertising in a future version.

Hosting of this support website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors sign in to GitHub. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or forms to this website. This section describes visits to the support website; the offline app does not automatically connect to this website.
