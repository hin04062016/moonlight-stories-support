Frequently asked questions

01How do I listen to a whole story?

Open a book and tap Read Entire Story. Narration starts on the first page and turns pages automatically through the end. Use the central play button to pause or resume. Turning a page manually or leaving the reader stops whole-story playback.

02How do I change the narrator?

Tap Voices in the reader, then choose Achernar, Aoede, or Charon. You can preview the current page. Your selection is saved automatically.

03How do I change language or use dark mode?

Tap the globe and choose Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, or English. The interface, story text, and narration change together, while favorites and reading progress are preserved. The same screen offers light, dark, or system appearance.

04How do I adjust music and sound effects?

Tap Background Music in the library or Voices in a story. Music and scene sounds have separate switches and volume controls. Music becomes quieter during narration. After unplugging headphones, tap play or resume music to continue.

05How do I find another story?

Search by title or browse Bedtime, Adventure, and Friendship in Categories. Tap Random Story to open a randomly selected book, or tap a heart to save a favorite.

06Do I need an internet connection or an account to read?

After downloading the app, included stories, illustrations, and narration work offline. Reading does not require a user account.

07Where are my favorites and reading progress saved?

Favorites, reading progress, and preferences are stored on your current device. Cross-device sync is not available. Deleting the app may remove this local data.
